Your daily movement plan

7-Day Morning Core Routine

6–10 minutes each morning · Stability, strength, and recovery

Start with 60 seconds of easy movement. Complete one set of each exercise, resting 15–30 seconds as needed.

How to use this plan

Tap an exercise name to open step-by-step instructions and a form reminder. Check off exercises as you finish them. Your progress is saved in this browser and automatically starts fresh each Monday.

The weekday tabs are navigable at any time. Your selected day is not changed automatically while the page is open.

Four-week progression

Week 1: Learn technique, one easy set.
Week 2: Improve control and consistency.
Week 3: Add 2 repetitions or 5 seconds where comfortable.
Week 4: Add a little resistance or a second set on selected days.

Keep movements slow and comfortable. Stop a provoking exercise if you develop radiating leg pain, tingling, numbness, or worsening back pain. On low-energy mornings, even one exercise maintains the habit. This is a general exercise plan, not a substitute for individualized physical therapy.
